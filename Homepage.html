Common questions

How does Camprise connect to the tools we already use?
Camprise ships with native integrations for the systems most enterprises run on — Slack, Microsoft 365, NetSuite, Salesforce, Workday, Jira, GitHub, Snowflake, and more. Each integration is OAuth-scoped to exactly the read and write permissions your IT team approves, and every call is logged.
What is the implementation timeline?
Most teams ship their first production agent within two weeks. A typical rollout looks like: week one, your platform admin connects core systems and imports policies; week two, a functional lead designs and pilots the first agent end-to-end. From there, new agents take hours, not weeks.
How do we keep our data inside our own perimeter?
Camprise runs in your cloud or in our SOC 2-audited environment, your choice. Agents never train on your data, and every action they take is policy-checked at runtime against the rules your security team has signed off on.
Who in our company should own this?
A small platform team owns the connections, policies, and audit log. Functional leaders own their own agents. Camprise is built so the people closest to the work design the agents — not a central AI team.
How is Camprise priced?
Per seat for designers and approvers, plus usage-based pricing on agent runs. We publish a transparent rate card and offer annual commitments with predictable ceilings.
